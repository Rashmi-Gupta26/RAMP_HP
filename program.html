The programme

From Trade to Venture — Transforming the Rural MSME Ecosystem.

The Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP) programme is a World Bank–assisted initiative of the Ministry of MSME, designed to strengthen Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises across India. In Himachal Pradesh it is executed by the Department of Industries, with IIT Mandi Catalyst as the state's Knowledge Partner and Technology Business Incubator.

Programme phases

Journey of learning
Phase I
Awareness & Introduction
Phase I helped students and teachers learn about startups, business ideas and entrepreneurship.
  • Reached 1,000+ students
  • Connected with 30+ teachers
  • Programmes in Mandi, Kullu and Lahaul & Spiti
Goal — encourage young people to explore business and self-employment opportunities.
Phase II
Learning to Start a Business
Phase II helps students turn their skills and ideas into real businesses and self-employment opportunities.
  • Skill development training
  • Practical startup bootcamps
  • Business guidance and mentorship
  • Support for creating small businesses and startups
Goal — build confidence to create jobs and grow ventures.

Core interventions

How we deliver
Intervention
Sensitization workshops
Simple workshops in colleges, ITIs and villages to introduce students to entrepreneurship. Participants learn:
  • What startups are
  • Career opportunities in business
  • Government and industry support available
These workshops encourage people to think creatively and take their first step.
Intervention
Entrepreneurship Development Programme (EDP)
  • Digital marketing — SEO basics, AI tools, Canva, Mailchimp, Google Analytics.
  • Financial literacy — cash flow, profits, expenses and schemes like HPCED and HIM Startup Yojana.
  • Business Model Canvas — a step-by-step framework to turn ideas into scalable plans.
Intervention
Bootcamp phases
  • Phase A — Idea formulation: develop business ideas and solve local problems through practical activities.
  • Phase B — IIT Mandi exposure visit: tour Drone, Robotics and Design Practicum labs, and meet founders like Pankaj Singh and Sachin Korla.
