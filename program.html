The programme

From Trade to Venture—transforming the rural MSME ecosystem.

The Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP) programme is a World Bank–assisted initiative of the Ministry of MSME, designed to strengthen Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises across India. In Himachal Pradesh it is executed by the Department of Industries, with IIT Mandi Catalyst as the state's Knowledge Partner and Technology Business Incubator.

Phase I — Awareness & Introduction

Phase I helped students and teachers learn about startups, business ideas, and Entrepreneurship

- Reached 1000+ students.

- Connected with 30+ teachers

- Conducted programmes in Mandi, Kullu and Lahaul & Spiti.

The main goal was to encourage young people to explore business and self-employment opportunities

Phase II — Learning to start Business

Phase II focuses on helping students turn their skills and ideas into real business and self employment opportunities

- Skill development training.

- Practical startup bootcamps.

- Business guidance and mentorship

support for creating small business and startups

This phase helps students become confident enough to create jobs and grows their own businesses and startups

Entrepreneurship Development Programme (EDP)

  • Digital marketing — Learn SEO basics, AI tools, Canva, Mailchimp, and Google Analytics to promote and expand businesses online.
  • Financial literacy —Understand cash flow, profits, expenses, and government schemes like HPCED and HIM Startup Yojana to manage business finances better.
  • Business Model Canvas — Learn a simple step-by-step framework to turn ideas into structured business plans and scalable startups. .

Bootcamp phases

  • Phase A — Idea formulation Students learn how to develop business ideas, solve local problems, and create simple startup plans through practical activities and workshops.
  • Phase B — IIT Mandi exposure visit: Selected participants visit IIT Mandi to explore innovation labs like Drone, Robotics, and Design Practicum labs. Participants also interact with successful founders and mentors such as Pankaj Singh and Sachin Korla to learn how to build and grow startups successfully.

The role of IIT Mandi Catalyst

As the state's premier Technology Business Incubator and Knowledge Partner, IIT Mandi Catalyst sets implementation standards, designs curriculum, runs pre-incubation centres, and conducts grassroots outreach in remote areas such as Lahaul & Spiti.

Way forward

  • Establishing 36 pre-incubation and rural incubation centres.
  • Implementing a Winter Incubation Protocol using hybrid mentoring for high-altitude centres.
  • Creating alumni–SHG linkages between ITI graduates (technical labour) and SHGs (resource owners).
  • Leveraging the District Industries Centre (DIC) for micro-grants to mitigate funding delays.
