How RAMP works

An execution-first programme — sensitization to venture creation.

Phase I sensitized 1,000+ students and 30+ faculty across Mandi, Kullu and Lahaul & Spiti. Phase II shifts toward intensive capacity-building, sector-specific bootcamps and venture creation.

Stage 01

Awareness

We come to your ITI, college or village to introduce the startup ecosystem and the fundamentals of entrepreneurship.

Stage 02

Bootcamp

Practical training in business, marketing and finance - 3 cohorts running.

Stage 03

Incubation

Mentorship and lab support to turn ideas into startups - 11 ideas prototyped

Where we work

49+ centres across 9 districts of Himachal Pradesh.

Each district hosts a network of ITIs, polytechnics, government colleges and senior secondary schools — supported by four implementing agencies, with IIT Mandi Catalyst as Knowledge Partner.
